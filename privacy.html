Privacy

Short version: this website collects nothing about you or your child.

No data collection

tibinokids.com has no contact forms, no newsletter sign-up, no comments, no accounts and no analytics or advertising trackers. We do not set cookies. We never ask for a name, an email address, an age or any other personal detail — from adults or from children.

Children's privacy

Our content is made for children under 13. In line with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the GDPR, we do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Because we collect nothing at all, there is nothing for a parent to review, correct or delete. If you still have a question, please contact us at the address below.

Embedded YouTube videos

Episodes on this site are embedded through youtube-nocookie.com, YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode. YouTube does not store cookies for personalised advertising until a video is played. When a video is played, or when you follow a link to our channel, YouTube's own privacy policy applies. All our videos are published as Made for Kids, which switches off personalised advertising and comments on YouTube's side.

Fonts

This site loads two typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser contacts Google's servers to fetch them, which means your IP address is visible to Google for that request. No cookies are set for this.

Hosting

The site is delivered as static files. The hosting provider may keep short-lived technical server logs for security and troubleshooting; these are not used to build a profile and are not linked to any person by us.

Contact

Questions about this page or about our content? Write to hello@tibinokids.com.
