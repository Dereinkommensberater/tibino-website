Is Tibino Kids safe for toddlers?

The short answer is yes — and here is exactly what that means, so you can decide for yourself instead of taking our word for it.

Tibino Kids is made for children aged 2 to 6. Every episode is free of violence, jump scares, sudden loud noises and frightening characters. Problems are solved with kindness, help always arrives, and every story ends calmly and happily. Episodes are published as Made for Kids on YouTube, which switches off personalised advertising and comments.

What never goes into an episode

  • No violence and no villains. Nobody is a bad guy. When something goes wrong, it goes wrong by accident — a basket drifts away, someone gets lost — and the story is about putting it right.
  • No jump scares and no sudden noises. The sound stays in a narrow, soft range. There is no moment designed to startle a child who is watching quietly.
  • No darkness used for tension. Night scenes are lit warmly. Fear is never the tool we use to hold attention.
  • No cliffhanger that leaves a child anxious. An episode may end with a gentle open question — what is Milo thinking? — but never with someone in danger.
  • No fast cutting or flashing. Shots are held long enough for a two-year-old to understand what they are looking at.
  • Nothing to buy, nothing to click. No merchandise pitches, no in-video calls to action beyond a friendly goodbye.

What we put in instead

  • A problem a child can graspSomeone needs help. That is the whole plot — no subplots, no twists, no misdirection.
  • Help that arrivesThe friends notice, they act, and it works. Children this age are learning that the world responds when you ask for help.
  • A calm endingThe last scene is quiet: everyone together, warm light, a happy sound. Good for the moment before a nap or bedtime.
  • Faces that stay the sameEvery character is designed once and kept visually identical across all episodes, so children recognise their friends immediately.

What happens on the platform

All our videos are marked Made for Kids on YouTube. That setting is not cosmetic — it switches off personalised advertising, comments, notifications and several data-collection features on YouTube's side. For the calmest experience we recommend watching in the YouTube Kids app or with supervised playback, so a finished episode does not roll on into something you have not chosen.

What happens on this website

Nothing is collected here. There is no contact form, no newsletter, no comments, no account, no analytics and no advertising cookies. The episode on the homepage only contacts YouTube once you press play — before that, no request leaves this page. You can read the details in our privacy notice.

Who makes it

A small independent animation team. There is no licensing pressure and no toy line behind the stories, which means an episode only exists when it has something gentle to say.
