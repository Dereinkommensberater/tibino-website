“I am Foxy. I used to play all by myself. Now I have friends and that is much better.”

Where Foxy lives

The path from the barn to the apple tree

Foxy turns up from the direction of the orchard, along the dirt path that runs from the red barn to the old apple tree. Nobody is quite sure where he sleeps, and he likes it that way.

About Foxy

Foxy is a small chubby fox cub who always stands upright on two legs and has two arms with little paw-hands. Orange fur, cream muzzle, cream chest, a fluffy tail with a cream tip and large green eyes.

He looks mischievous and never threatening. Everything about him is soft.

What Foxy is like

  • Shows off before saying hello
    Attention is how he asks for friendship.
  • Seems mysterious, is simply lonely
    He was on his own for a long time before the others found him.
  • Loyal the moment he is taken seriously
    Underneath all the tricks, he just wants to belong.
  • Drops the apple when spoken to
    And catches it again at the very last moment. Every time.
