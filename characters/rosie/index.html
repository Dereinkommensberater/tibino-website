Rosie

The bouncy piglet

Rosie is a tiny pink piglet with a curly tail that wiggles when she is excited, which is most of the time.

“I am Rosie. I am loud and I am bouncy and I love mud the most.”

Say hello to Rosie

Fifteen seconds. Nothing is loaded until you press play.

Where Rosie lives

The pond behind the barn

Rosie can be found wherever the ground is softest — usually the muddy edge of the pond behind the barn, and reliably there within a minute of any rain.

About Rosie

Rosie is small, pink and round, with a snub snout and a tail curled like a spring. She is the loudest friend on the farm by a wide margin.

She bounces rather than walks.

What Rosie is like

  • Loud and entirely unbothered by it
    Rosie has never once been quiet on purpose.
  • Believes puddles are for sharing
    And she will invite everybody, whether they want to or not.
  • Happiest just after rain
    The others have learned to recognise that particular squeal.
  • Forgives instantly
    She cannot hold on to a mood for longer than a minute.

Every episode is made for this age. No villains, no jump scares, no sudden loud noises and no cliffhangers — the full list is on what we never show.
