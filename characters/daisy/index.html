Daisy

The gentle cow

Daisy is the biggest friend on the farm and by far the calmest.

“I am Daisy. If you ever feel small, come and stand beside me. There is always room here for you.”

Say hello to Daisy

Fifteen seconds. Nothing is loaded until you press play.

Where Daisy lives

The cattle gate at the foot of the gravel path

Daisy stands at the wooden gate where the gravel path meets the meadow. It is the spot with the best view of the whole farm, and she is almost always there.

About Daisy

Daisy has a white coat with warm brown patches, soft brown eyes and a small yellow flower tucked behind one ear.

Her moo is the deepest sound anywhere on the farm, and she knows it.

What Daisy is like

  • Never hurries anybody
    She simply stands there until you feel better.
  • The one small animals go to
    When somebody little needs somebody big, they come to Daisy.
  • Patient beyond reason
    Nothing on this farm has ever made her rush.
  • Says less than the others
    And is listened to more.
