Where Woolly lives

The open meadow above the farm

Woolly belongs to the open meadow that rises behind the farm. It is also the easiest place in the world to lose sight of a very small lamb, which is more or less the story of his life.

About Woolly

Woolly is a small cream-coloured lamb with soft curly wool and grey hooves. He is the littlest of everybody, including Pip, if you do not count the feathers.

His bleat is high and thin, and it carries a surprisingly long way.

What Woolly is like

  • Gets lost more than anyone
    Not from wandering off. From stopping to look at things.
  • Never frightened for long
    Because somebody always comes.
  • Trusts completely
    He has no reason yet not to.
  • Shakes out his wool when happy
    Which is how you can tell from a distance.

Every episode is made for this age. No villains, no jump scares, no sudden loud noises and no cliffhangers — the full list is on what we never show.
