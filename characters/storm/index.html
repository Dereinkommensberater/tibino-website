Characters

Storm

The brave puppy

Storm is a round golden puppy with ears that fly out sideways when he runs. He runs almost everywhere.

“I am Storm. I run the fastest on the whole farm, but my favourite thing is sitting right beside you.”

Say hello to Storm

Fifteen seconds. Nothing is loaded until you press play.

Where Storm lives

The open yard in front of the barn

Storm has no fixed corner. Wherever somebody is, that is where he is — usually the open yard between the barn and the house, because from there he can see everyone at once.

About Storm

His fur is warm honey-gold with a paler cream belly, and his tail is thick enough to wag with his whole body. He wears nothing at all: no collar, no lead, no bandana.

He is the fastest on the farm and the first to notice when something is wrong.

What Storm is like

  • Helps before he thinks
    When a kitten was drifting down the river, Storm went into the water without stopping.
  • Never still for long
    Except when somebody sits down next to him. Then he can be still for hours.
  • Ears say everything
    Up means listening, flat means sorry, flying means running.
  • Gentle with the little ones
    For all his speed, he slows right down around Pip and Milo.
