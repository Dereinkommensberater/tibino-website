Pip

The little chick

Pip is the smallest one on the farm and Tibino's best friend. He is very fast, very fluffy and just a little bit nervous about everything.

“I am Pip. I am very small and a little bit shy, but when a friend needs me, I am always brave.”

Say hello to Pip

Fifteen seconds. Nothing is loaded until you press play.

Where Pip lives

The henhouse at first light

Pip sleeps in the henhouse and is usually awake before anyone else. He is out on the step waiting when Tibino's door opens, which is why he is in almost every story.

About Pip

Pip is a tiny round golden-yellow chick with soft down, an orange beak and orange feet. He fits comfortably in Tibino's hands.

His eyebrows are made of feathers, which means you can always tell exactly what he is feeling.

What Pip is like

  • Frightened first, loyal anyway
    He flinches, and then he stays. Every time.
  • Borrowed courage
    As long as Tibino is there, Pip will try anything.
  • The first to cheer
    When something turns out well, his is the first voice you hear.
  • Rides on a shoulder
    And flutters up at every surprise before settling straight back down.
